About ETracker

A clearer picture of student spending.

ETracker is a student-focused expense tracker that brings transactions from mobile money and bank statements into one easy-to-understand view.

◎

Why we built it

Statements list transactions, but rarely show the patterns behind them. ETracker helps make everyday spending and month-to-month changes easier to see.

⌂

Who it's for

Built for students who juggle an allowance, side income, mobile money and bank accounts—and want a quick, straightforward way to understand their money.

▤

How the website helps

Import a statement to bring transactions together, review account activity, and use simple summaries and charts to spot where your money is going.

✓

Your data, your choice

Imported transaction data is used to prepare your spending summaries. You can choose a data-retention period in Settings; this prototype does not connect to financial accounts.
